- RSU sends I2V_YIELD broadcast while emergency waits.
- V0 is granted before normal vehicles after the intersection becomes free.

Fairness result:
- V5 is eventually granted.
- This shows that starvation is avoided.

Safety result:

- No collision alarm appears.
